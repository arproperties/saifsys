# Tenancy Contract v2 – translation sheet

For the legal translator. Arabic prevails over English (see the clause "In the event of any discrepancy… the Arabic text shall prevail"), so every clause below needs an approved Arabic text before v2 is given to tenants.

- Clause numbers are the v2 numbers with the default settings (renewal fee clause included, no guarantor).
- The Guarantor clause is printed only on leases that have a guarantor. It then sits after clause 29, and the clauses after it move down by one.
- Words in `{{…}}` are filled in from the lease when the contract is printed (e.g. `{{MAX_OCCUPANTS}}` → 4). Keep them as they are in the Arabic text.
- In the template, each changed clause shows `[AR – TRANSLATION PENDING – Clause N]` in the Arabic column. The old Arabic text is kept next to it in an HTML comment.
- Clauses not listed here are unchanged and keep their existing Arabic text.

## Clause 1
*amended — old clause 1*

The Tenant, upon signing this Lease Agreement, acknowledges having received the Leased Premises and its appurtenances in the condition recorded in the signed Move-In Inspection Report attached as Annex B, which both Parties acknowledge as the agreed record of the condition of the Leased Premises at handover, in accordance with the terms set forth herein. The Tenant further undertakes to return the Leased Premises to the Landlord or their authorized representative upon the expiration or termination of the lease term, in the same condition as received, subject to normal wear and tear resulting from ordinary use. This clause is in accordance with Law No. (26) of 2007 Regulating the Relationship between Landlords and Tenants in the Emirate of Dubai, and its amendments.

## Clause 4
*replaced — old clause 4*

**Maintenance and repairs.**

(a) Tenant's responsibility: (i) minor maintenance and repairs of the Leased Premises where the cost of an individual repair incident, as assessed by the Landlord's maintenance team or a contractor approved by the Landlord, is below AED {{MINOR_REPAIR_THRESHOLD}} ({{MINOR_REPAIR_THRESHOLD_WORDS}} Dirhams); (ii) all maintenance, repair and eventual removal of any alterations, additions or installations made by the Tenant; (iii) maintenance of any non-original fitting that the Tenant has expressly accepted in Annex B; and (iv) irrespective of cost, repair of any damage caused by the Tenant's misuse or negligence, by persons the Tenant admits to the Leased Premises, or by the Tenant's failure to report a defect promptly.

(b) Landlord's responsibility: (i) any individual repair incident costing AED {{MINOR_REPAIR_THRESHOLD}} ({{MINOR_REPAIR_THRESHOLD_WORDS}} Dirhams) or more, in full and not only the excess; (ii) all structural works, waterproofing, main electrical, plumbing and drainage infrastructure, district cooling / chiller equipment and any building-wide system, irrespective of cost; and (iii) any non-original fitting not listed in Annex B as accepted by the Tenant.

(c) Reporting: the Tenant shall report any defect or damage through the 'Ain Al Reem Living' application or in writing within forty-eight (48) hours of becoming aware of it. Where a delay in reporting increases the cost of repair, the additional cost shall be borne by the Tenant.

(d) Repairs within the Tenant's responsibility that the Tenant fails to carry out within a reasonable time may be carried out by the Landlord and charged to the Tenant at cost.

## Clause 5
*amended — old clause 5*

On the handover date, both Parties shall jointly complete and sign the Move-In Inspection Report (Annex B), recording the condition of each room and fitting with dated photographs, the meter readings (DEWA, cooling, gas) and a list of any non-original fittings and whether the Tenant accepts their maintenance. A Move-Out Inspection Report (Annex C) shall be completed jointly on the day the keys are returned, following the same room order. Where the Tenant declines to attend or sign, the Landlord's report, witnessed by a second representative and sent to the Tenant's notice address the same day, shall be the record. Both the Landlord and the Tenant shall retain a copy of these reports and commit to documenting any remarks or modifications to the condition of the leased premises throughout the lease term.

## Clause 12
*replaced — old clause 12*

**Rent payment and returned cheques.**

(a) The Tenant shall pay the rent on the due dates set out in the Payment Schedule (Annex A). The cheque dates in Annex A are the due dates of the respective instalments.

(b) If any cheque is returned unpaid for a reason attributable to the Tenant, the Tenant shall reimburse the Landlord's actual, documented costs arising from the return (including bank charges and the cost of serving notices). A cheque returned because the account is closed, the signature does not match or the amounts in words and figures differ shall be treated as a material breach.

(c) The Landlord's acceptance of a replacement cheque or of a partial payment is on account only and does not extend any due date, waive any notice already served, or waive any right of the Landlord.

(d) In the event of non-payment, the Landlord may exercise its rights under Article 25 of Law No. 26 of 2007 as amended, including service of the statutory notice through the notary public or registered mail, and may claim rent until the date the Leased Premises are actually vacated and handed over.

## Clause 13
*amended — old clause 13*

The lease agreement shall not be considered effective nor delivered to the Tenant until the first rent installment cheque is cleared by the bank and the Tenant has submitted all documents required by the Landlord or his authorized representative. Failure to fulfill these conditions entitles the Landlord to withhold delivery of the lease agreement or suspend its effectiveness until full compliance is met. The Landlord shall register the contract with Ejari within {{EJARI_REGISTRATION_DAYS_WORDS}} ({{EJARI_REGISTRATION_DAYS}}) working days after the first instalment has cleared and the Tenant's documents have been received. Nothing in this clause entitles the Landlord to withhold Ejari registration once those conditions are met.

## Clause 15
*replaced (old 15 + old 18 merged) — old clauses 15 and 18 (merged)*

**Renewal and changes of terms.**

(a) Either Party wishing to amend the rent or any term for the following lease period shall notify the other Party in writing at least ninety (90) days before the expiry date. Any rent increase shall be within the limits of the RERA Rental Index applicable at the time.

(b) If no such notice is given, and the Tenant remains in occupation, the contract renews for a like period on the same terms, in accordance with Article 6 of Law No. 26 of 2007 as amended.

(c) The Landlord may decline to renew only on the grounds and subject to the notice periods and methods prescribed by Article 25(2) of Law No. 26 of 2007 as amended.

(d) A Tenant who does not wish to renew shall notify the Landlord in writing at least ninety (90) days before the expiry date to allow re-letting; where notice is given later, the Tenant remains liable for rent until the expiry date or the actual handover date, whichever is later, and the Landlord shall use reasonable efforts to re-let.

## Clause 17
*amended (extended) — old clause 17*

(a) The Tenant shall not sublease the leased premises, whether in whole or in part, nor assign or permit any third party to occupy or use the premises in any manner, whether for consideration or otherwise, without the prior express written consent of the Landlord. The Tenant is strictly prohibited from using the leased premises for any purpose other than the residential purpose stated in this Agreement. In the event of a breach of this provision, the Landlord shall have the right to take all necessary legal actions, including immediate eviction and claiming damages. Furthermore, the Tenant undertakes to provide the Landlord or his authorized representative with copies of valid identification or residency documents for all individuals residing with the Tenant in the leased premises, within seven (7) days from the date of their occupancy, for security and regulatory purposes.

(b) Occupancy: the Leased Premises shall be occupied only by the Tenant and the persons listed in Annex B, not exceeding {{MAX_OCCUPANTS}} persons, as {{OCCUPANCY_TYPE}}. Use as a holiday home, short-term accommodation, shared or bachelor accommodation, or any use contrary to the regulations of the competent authority for the area (including Trakhees or Dubai Municipality) is prohibited. Any fine or penalty imposed on the Landlord as a result of the Tenant's use shall be reimbursed by the Tenant within seven (7) days of demand.

(c) Authority to consent: no consent, approval or no-objection certificate binds the Landlord unless it is issued on the Landlord's letterhead, bears a reference number and is signed by an authorised signatory named in the Landlord's trade licence or a notarised power of attorney. Real-estate brokers, agents and building staff have no authority to issue consents or documents on the Landlord's behalf.

## Clause 18
*amended — old clause 19*

The Tenant shall bear full responsibility for the payment of all governmental, municipal, and housing fees, as well as the registration fees of the Lease Agreement with the Real Estate Regulatory Agency (RERA), whether such fees are currently due or may become due in the future, in relation to the Leased Premises throughout the lease term. The Tenant shall also be responsible for the timely settlement of all utility bills including water, electricity, sewage, district cooling / chiller charges, gas, telecommunications and internet, and any other service charges related to the Leased Premises. Any delay or failure in payment shall be deemed a breach of the Tenant’s contractual obligations.

## Clause 20
*replaced — old clause 21*

**Security deposit.**

(a) Within seven (7) working days after the Move-Out Inspection Report (Annex C) is completed, the Landlord shall send the Tenant an itemised deduction statement, each item referenced to Annex B and Annex C with photographs and the repair cost or invoice.

(b) The Tenant may object in writing within five (5) working days of receipt. The undisputed balance of the deposit shall be refunded within twenty-five (25) working days after the Tenant has provided final clearance certificates for DEWA, district cooling / chiller, gas, and telecommunications / internet, and has returned all keys and access cards.

(c) No deduction shall be made for normal wear and tear, which includes minor scuffs and marks, small nail holes, fading from sunlight, and ordinary repainting after a tenancy of twelve (12) months or more unless specific damage is recorded.

(d) The Landlord may deduct unpaid rent, unpaid utility charges, the cost of replacing unreturned keys or access cards, and the cost of repairing damage beyond normal wear and tear proven by Annex B and Annex C.

## Clause 26
*amended — old clause 27*

In the event the Tenant breaches any of the terms stipulated in this Agreement, the Landlord shall notify the Tenant in writing to remedy the breach within a period of no less than thirty (30) days from the date of such notice, served in accordance with Article 25(1)(h) of Law No. 26 of 2007 as amended. If the Tenant fails to rectify the breach within this period, the Landlord shall have the right to terminate the Agreement and demand evacuation of the leased premises, holding the Tenant liable for all damages and losses arising from such breach. The Landlord shall also be entitled to take all necessary legal actions to enforce his rights, including claiming compensation and legal costs, in accordance with the provisions of this Agreement and the applicable laws of the Emirate of Dubai.

## Clause 27
*new - Rent until actual vacating — new clause*

Rent at the contract rate continues to accrue until the Tenant returns all keys and access cards and the Move-Out Inspection Report (Annex C) is signed or issued, including any period of occupation after expiry or termination. Any such period shall be charged pro rata on a daily basis.
